Our Passion for Intramuros

We’re a group of students who spent the last two months turning our vision into reality, sharing the beauty of Intramuros with everyone.

Our Mission

To capture and showcase the timeless charm of Intramuros, inviting both locals and visitors to explore and appreciate its hidden corners.

Our Vision

We envision an engaged community of explorers who discover, review, and celebrate the vibrant tapestry of Intramuros’ culture and heritage.
