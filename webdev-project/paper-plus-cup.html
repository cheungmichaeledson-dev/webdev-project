Paper + Cup

A creative escape in the heart of Intramuros—sip, read, and reflect in this charming book café.

Overview

Paper + Cup is a charming café and independent bookstore nestled inside the historic walled city of Intramuros. With rustic wooden shelves, cozy seating, and the aroma of freshly brewed coffee, this café invites visitors to slow down, read a book, or get creative.

Run by The Manila Collectible Co., the space serves as both a coffee nook and a cultural hub, often hosting workshops, local artisan pop-ups, and intimate gatherings. Whether you're visiting for a caffeine fix, to browse locally published titles, or to journal in peace—Paper + Cup is a hidden gem worth finding.

📚 What Makes It Special

  • Book Café Ambiance: Sip quality coffee while reading or writing in a peaceful, artsy space.
  • Locally Made Goods: The café stocks local zines, books, stationery, and artisanal products.

  • Instagram-worthy Interiors: A warm, vintage aesthetic perfect for photos and aesthetic journaling.
  • Quiet Retreat in the City: Tucked inside The Manila Collectible Co., away from the crowds.
